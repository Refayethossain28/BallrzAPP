№ 09 — Questions, answered

Sensible questions, straight answers.

Whereabouts in South West London?

I travel to pupils across South West London. Get in touch with your postcode and we’ll see if it works — and if it doesn’t, online lessons work beautifully.

How do online lessons work?

A video call plus a shared digital whiteboard. Your child needs a laptop or tablet, a pencil and some paper — that’s it.

What do parents actually get?

A short note after each session: what we covered, what clicked, and what’s next. You’ll never have to guess how it’s going.

How much are lessons?

Fees depend on stage and format, so there’s no price list here. Send a message and I’ll talk you through it — no obligation, no hard sell.

What happens in the first lesson?

Some talking and a little maths: finding what’s solid, what’s wobbly, and what we’ll do about it — with at least one genuinely surprising bit of maths along the way.
