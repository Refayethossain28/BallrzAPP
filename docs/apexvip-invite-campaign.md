# ApexVIP — "By Invitation Only" launch campaign

**The idea:** ApexVIP does not launch. It *admits*. No download ads, no "sign up
free" — one hundred founding memberships, personally invited, then a waiting
list. Scarcity is the product before the product: every asset says *you cannot
simply have this*, which is precisely what makes people want it.

The app already carries the machinery this campaign needs: **Charter Member
numbers** (shown on the booking confirmation), **referral codes**
(`generateReferralCode` — each member holds two "invitations"), the **Velvet
members-only travel desk**, and the APEX loyalty ledger. The campaign simply
puts a velvet rope in front of what's built.

---

## Phase 0 · The Silence — weeks 1–2

No product. No features. No prices. Black posts only.

- **Organic (IG + TikTok):** the invite teaser film (`invite-teaser.mp4`) and
  the black statics (`post-invite.jpg`, `story-invite.jpg`). Captions of five
  words or fewer: *"Not for everyone."* · *"London. Soon."* · *"One hundred."*
- **No bio link yet.** The account exists, follows no one, replies to no one.
  Comments asking "what is this" go unanswered — the audience does the
  marketing.
- **Seed list:** 20–30 personal contacts receive the invitation card
  (`invite-card.jpg`) by WhatsApp/DM with their code. These are the first
  members and the first word-of-mouth.

## Phase 1 · The Hundred — weeks 3–6

The velvet rope opens exactly one hundred times.

- **The offer (Founding Membership):** member number engraved in the app
  (Charter Member #1–100), founding rate locked for life, Velvet concierge
  privileges — the travel desk that books flights and stays — and **two
  invitations of their own** (their referral code). People don't join a
  service; they join a number.
- **Distribution:** invitation card DMs only. Every post keeps the same close:
  *"By invitation only."* The bio link goes live → apexvip.uk with promo code
  entry (invitation code = referral code).
- **Paid (small, precise):** the piano/cello-scored advert and the desire
  statics, geo-targeted London (Mayfair, Chelsea, Knightsbridge, Canary
  Wharf), interests: private aviation, five-star hotels, luxury watches.
  Paid uses the original scores (cleared — composed in-house).
- **Content cadence:** 3 posts/week — one teaser, one black card, one desire
  asset. Stories weekly with a "𝟏𝟖 𝐨𝐟 𝟏𝟎𝟎 𝐫𝐞𝐦𝐚𝐢𝐧" counter (update by hand;
  scarcity must be true — count real signups from the admin CRM).

## Phase 2 · The Waiting List — week 7 onward

The hundred are seated. Now everyone else queues — politely.

- Public CTA changes to **"Request an invitation"** → apexvip.uk. Requests
  land in the admin CRM pipeline (lead stages already exist) and are admitted
  in monthly batches, announced as *"Admissions open: twelve places."*
- Members' two invitations become the growth engine — referred members skip
  the list, referrers earn APEX. Post occasional *"Invited by a member"*
  stories (with permission, initials only).
- The line for everything public: **"ApexVIP — by invitation, or by
  patience."**

## Copy bank

Teasers: *Not for everyone.* · *One hundred founding members.* · *By
invitation only.* · *London. Soon.* · *You'll know if you're invited.*
Turn lines: *It isn't a dream. It's a membership.* · *By invitation, or by
patience.* Close: *Arrive differently.* — always with **apexvip.uk** in gold.

## Mechanics — what maps to what in the product

| Campaign piece | Already in the app |
|---|---|
| Invitation code | Referral code (`generateReferralCode` / `applyReferralCode`) |
| Charter Member #N | `memberNumber`, shown on booking confirmation |
| Founding privileges | Velvet membership → members-only travel desk |
| Waiting list / admissions | Admin CRM lead pipeline (stages) |
| Scarcity counter truth | Admin dashboard client count |

## KPIs

Phase 0: reach + profile visits (no follower target — mystery over mass).
Phase 1: 100 founding members, ≥30% from personal invitations, CAC on paid.
Phase 2: waiting-list size, invitation-conversion rate, member referral share.

## Rules

- Never show a price in public. Never say "app". Say *membership*.
- Every asset ends in gold: **apexvip.uk**.
- The films contain photorealistic AI-generated people — keep the platform's
  **AI-content label ON** for every post.
- The scarcity must be real: if 100 are gone, close admissions until Phase 2.
