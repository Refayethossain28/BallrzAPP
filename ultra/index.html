What this is: a real MIPS R4300i integer-ISA interpreter with branch delay slots, HI/LO, big-endian RDRAM, an RGBA5551 framebuffer scanned out by a simplified VI, and the genuine N64 controller button layout. The bundled cartridges are homebrew MIPS programs assembled by the built-in assembler. What it isn't (yet): there is no RSP, RDP (3D), FPU or TLB — commercial ROM dumps are identified and loaded, but halt at the first instruction outside the integer subset, and the debugger tells you which one.
