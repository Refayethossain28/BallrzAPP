Loads any N64 ROM you have the right to use — your own dumps or freely-distributable homebrew. Ultra runs the R4300i integer core, so commercial titles boot but halt at their first RSP/RDP/FPU instruction.

Homebrew library

This searches Ultra's bundled cartridges and a curated list of open-source homebrew. Ultra does not search for or download commercial game ROMs — those are copyrighted. Community entries open a web search for that title's own legal release, which you can then paste into “Load a ROM” above.

Controller

Keyboard: arrow keys d-pad · X = A · Z = B · Enter = Start
Debugger — R4300i state
What this is: a real MIPS R4300i integer-ISA interpreter with branch delay slots, HI/LO, big-endian RDRAM, an RGBA5551 framebuffer scanned out by a simplified VI, and the genuine N64 controller button layout. The bundled cartridges are homebrew MIPS programs assembled by the built-in assembler. What it isn't (yet): there is no RSP, RDP (3D), FPU or TLB — commercial ROM dumps are identified and loaded, but halt at the first instruction outside the integer subset, and the debugger tells you which one.
