Pick a system, then load a ROM you own

Real libretro cores compiled to WebAssembly (EmulatorJS build), fully self-hosted — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Zipped ROMs (.zip / .7z) work too.

How this differs from the main Ultra page: the main page is a from-scratch CPU interpreter for learning — it runs homebrew and shows live disassembly. This page embeds complete, mature emulator cores (mupen64plus, snes9x, mGBA, Genesis Plus GX, PCSX-ReARMed, FinalBurn Neo, MAME 2003+ and more) so real games render and play.
Only load ROMs you have the legal right to use — your own cartridge and disc dumps. Nothing leaves your browser.
