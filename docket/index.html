How Docket works: everything stays on this device — photos and text live in your browser only. Docket reads the lines you give it, sorts the pile by what genuinely matters (deadlines, money at stake, escalation language), briefs you on each document, and drafts the letters — appeals, prove-it demands, disputes, cancellations — that deal with it. Its tips are practical starting points, not legal advice.
