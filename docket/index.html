How Docket works: everything stays on this device — photos and text live in your browser only. Docket reads the lines you give it, sorts the pile by what genuinely matters (deadlines, money at stake, escalation language), briefs you on each document, and drafts the letters — appeals, prove-it demands, disputes, cancellations — that deal with it.

Docket AI (optional): link your own Anthropic API key (“Link AI”, above) and the assistant gets a real brain — it reads the photo itself, fills in the details, judges scam risk, answers “what should I do?”, does tasks you type in plain English, and writes finished letters with the real names, amounts and dates filled in.
