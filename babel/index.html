📖 Phrasebook — tap a phrase, the wall answers. Zero typing needed.

How it works, honestly: everything on this page is deterministic logic running on your device — a hand-checked 40-phrase traveller phrasebook across 36 languages and 16 writing systems (typo-tolerant, reachable from any of them), real-grammar spelling of numbers, clock times and dates (French vigesimals, Georgian scores, Chinese 零-insertion, the lakh/crore grouping), and computed romanization of Cyrillic, Greek, Hangul, kana and Georgian. No model, no network, no account. Engine unit-tested by npm run test:babel.

🎙️ Live interpreter — tap a side and speak; Babel answers out loud in the other language.

Honest limits: the browser can't identify a language from raw audio, so the interpreter listens in the tapped side's language (Auto alternates turns and cross-checks each transcript with Babel's own 36-language detector). Speech recognition needs a connection on most phones — the Translate tab stays fully offline. Babel only speaks what it can honestly translate: its 40-phrase book, numbers, times and dates.
