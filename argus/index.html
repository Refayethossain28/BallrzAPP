Settings

Argus runs on public feeds straight from your browser. Two layers need a free key of their own; keys stay in this device's storage and are only ever sent to that provider.

OpenSky credits used today: 0 of 400. Argus polls a bounded box when you are zoomed in (cheaper) and backs off when the budget is gone.

Keys: 1–5 sensor modes · M mesh · T tour · G ground tracks · C console · L layers · R roster · +/− zoom · Esc release.

By design Argus has no people search, no face recognition and no way to follow an individual: contacts are vehicles and natural events, from sources anyone can read.
