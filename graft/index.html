Reality check

  • The ranges are the honest part. Every £/hr here is what people actually make, not what a course-seller claims. Your first weeks will sit at the bottom of the range.
  • Fast money and big money are different hustles. Delivery pays this week and never scales; a course pays nothing for months and might scale forever. The matcher weighs whichever you pick — most people should run one of each.
  • Tax: the first £1,000/yr of side income is covered by the UK trading allowance. Above that, register for Self Assessment. Renting a room has its own £7,500 allowance.
  • Anything promising guaranteed daily returns, crypto doubling, or "passive £10k/mo" is a scam. Real income is boring: hours, skill, repeat customers, invoices paid.
