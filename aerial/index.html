About

Aerial streams real media from the open internet with no backend of its own: live internet radio from the community Radio Browser directory, any podcast feed, and any HLS, MP4 or direct audio link you paste. The HLS player is built from scratch — playlist parser, adaptive bitrate, live refresh, MPEG-TS demuxer and fragmented-MP4 remuxer feeding Media Source Extensions. Safari plays HLS natively.

Plain limits

  • No MPEG-DASH (.mpd) manifests.
  • No SAMPLE-AES or DRM-protected streams; AES-128 segment keys are fine.
  • No subtitles or captions yet.
  • Podcast feeds must send a CORS header — Aerial has no proxy.
  • Plain http:// streams are blocked by the browser when Aerial runs on https://.

Credits

Station directory: Radio Browser. Podcast search: the iTunes Search API. Sample streams are test content published by Apple, Mux, Bitmovin, Unified Streaming, Akamai and Google — their owners hold all rights.
