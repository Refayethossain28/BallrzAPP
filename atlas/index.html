◮ Atlas — your own satnav. The map is drawn by a from-scratch canvas engine — with a 🛰 satellite layer (Esri World Imagery), a 3D drive view that tilts the world while you navigate, and your car as the position marker; guidance, snapping, phrasing and ETA come from the pure, unit-tested navigation engine (engine.js). Things no other satnav does: 🌅 sun-glare forecast (amber dashes on the route where you'll drive into a low sun), 🏁 co-driver rally pace notes, 🛰 tunnel-proof dead reckoning when GPS drops, a 📼 flight recorder (drive stats + GPX export, auto 🅿️ parked-car pin), a 📹 dashcam (record your drive with the in-app camera — time, position, speed and the next turn burned into every frame; clips live in an on-device vault with playback, native share-sheet export and honest auto-pruning — footage never leaves your device), 🍞 trail-back (retrace your own breadcrumbs, fully offline), an ⏱ ETA that learns your pace, 🛑 speed-limit + 📷 speed-camera warnings (OpenStreetMap data — only as complete as the map; always obey the signs on the road), 🚙 convoy mode — share a code and every car in your convoy appears live on the map (tabs sync instantly on one device; across devices via the optional cloud in config.js; only people with your code can see you, and beacons stop when you leave) — and 🚦 live traffic: real incidents (TfL's open feed across London; worldwide with a free TomTom key in config.js) snapped onto your routes with delay estimates and spoken warnings, plus a clearly-labelled typical rush-hour model shaping ETAs everywhere else. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors; routing by the community OSRM/FOSSGIS servers; search by Nominatim. Your location never leaves your device except to fetch the route you asked for.
