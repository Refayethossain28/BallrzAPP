◮ Atlas — your own satnav. The map is drawn by a from-scratch canvas engine — with a 🛰 satellite layer (Esri World Imagery), a 3D drive view that tilts the world while you navigate, 🏙 real 3D buildings (OSM footprints with real heights, extruded and light-shaded on-canvas), and your car as the position marker; guidance, snapping, phrasing and ETA come from the pure, unit-tested navigation engine (engine.js). 🎵 Music: import your own songs (stored on this device, playable offline) with shuffle/repeat, lock-screen controls — and the music ducks automatically while the satnav speaks, like a built-in car system. 🗺 Offline maps: save a route from its preview and its map corridor (day + night, zooms 12–17) downloads to this device — guidance, snapping and voice already run locally, so a saved route navigates with no signal; only re-routing and search still need a connection. Turn-by-turn includes 🛣 lane guidance (real OSM lane arrows, your lanes lit) and junction views — honest auto-generated schematics of complex junctions built from the real surrounding roads, your path glowing through them. Things no other satnav does: 🌅 sun-glare forecast (amber dashes on the route where you'll drive into a low sun), 🏁 co-driver rally pace notes, 🛰 tunnel-proof dead reckoning when GPS drops, a 📼 flight recorder (drive stats + GPX export, auto 🅿️ parked-car pin), a 📹 dashcam (record your drive with the in-app camera — time, position, speed and the next turn burned into every frame; clips live in an on-device vault with playback, native share-sheet export and honest auto-pruning — footage never leaves your device), 🍞 trail-back (retrace your own breadcrumbs, fully offline), an ⏱ ETA that learns your pace, 🛑 speed-limit + 📷 speed-camera warnings (OpenStreetMap data — only as complete as the map; always obey the signs on the road), 🚙 convoy mode — share a code and every car in your convoy appears live on the map (tabs sync instantly on one device; across devices via the optional cloud in config.js; only people with your code can see you, and beacons stop when you leave) — and 🚦 live traffic: real incidents (TfL's open feed across London; worldwide with a free TomTom key in config.js) snapped onto your routes with delay estimates and spoken warnings, plus a clearly-labelled typical rush-hour model shaping ETAs everywhere else. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors; routing by the community OSRM/FOSSGIS servers; search by Nominatim. Your location never leaves your device except to fetch the route you asked for.

🛡 Guardian

While you navigate, Atlas watches the phone's motion sensors for a crash — a violent impact followed by stillness. If it sees one it asks if you're OK, out loud. No answer in 30 seconds and it alerts your emergency contact with your position and a live map link. Everything runs on your phone; nothing is sent unless the countdown runs out.
