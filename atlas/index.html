◮ Atlas — your own satnav. The map is drawn by a from-scratch canvas engine; guidance, snapping, phrasing and ETA come from the pure, unit-tested navigation engine (engine.js). Things no other satnav does: 🌅 sun-glare forecast (amber dashes on the route where you'll drive into a low sun), 🏁 co-driver rally pace notes, 🛰 tunnel-proof dead reckoning when GPS drops, a 📼 flight recorder (drive stats + GPX export, auto 🅿️ parked-car pin), 🍞 trail-back (retrace your own breadcrumbs, fully offline), and an ⏱ ETA that learns your pace. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors; routing by the community OSRM/FOSSGIS servers; search by Nominatim. Your location never leaves your device except to fetch the route you asked for.
