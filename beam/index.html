Beam

Send a file to the phone next to you — with light. No Bluetooth, no Wi‑Fi, no cable, no account.

How it works
  1. The sender picks a file. Beam splits it into pieces and shows them as a fast loop of QR codes on its screen.
  2. The receiver points its camera at that screen. Every code it reads is a piece; pieces it misses are repaired by later codes, so nobody has to hold perfectly still.
  3. Done. The file is checked against its fingerprint, then saved or shared. Nothing ever leaves the two phones.

Honest speed: a few hundred KB a minute at best. Perfect for notes, contacts, documents and shrunk photos — not for videos. Beam shows you the estimate before you start.

Scan it with the other phone's normal camera app to open Beam there. It needs the internet once to load; after that it works offline.
