Furqan — ask the Qur’an

Answered only in its own words: verified text, cited by surah and ayah.
Nothing on this page is generated. Furqan finds and quotes the Qur’an — the Uthmani text of the King Fahd Complex with Pickthall’s translation, checksummed on load. It is a concordance with a good memory, not a scholar: it shows you what the Qur’an says on a subject; it does not issue rulings, and it will tell you when a question is not answered in the Qur’an at all.

What it is. A search engine over the whole Qur’an that works offline, with a curated map from the questions people ask to the passages scholars and readers turn to first. Every result is a real ayah at a real address; the page can only display strings that are in the verified text file it loaded.

What it is not. It is not a mufti and not a tafsir. Translations are interpretations of the Arabic; context, abrogation, the Sunnah and a thousand years of scholarship sit between a verse and a ruling. For anything that matters to your life, ask a qualified scholar.

Why no “AI model trained on the Qur’an”. A language model trained on scripture does not learn to answer questions; it learns to produce text in the same style — including verses that do not exist. That is the one failure a Qur’an app must never have, so Furqan never generates scripture. The optional Explain with Fable 5 step hands a frontier model the quoted verses and asks it to explain from those alone; its reply is then checked word by word, and anything it quotes that is not in the text is marked.

Sources. Arabic: Uthmani script, Hafs reading, from the King Fahd Glorious Qur’an Printing Complex, Madinah. Translation: Marmaduke Pickthall, The Meaning of the Glorious Koran (1930, public domain). Both via the fawazahmed0/quran-api mirror. Chapter names from the same table. Arabic typeface: Amiri Quran (SIL Open Font License), bundled so the Uthmani marks render on every device. For the Saheeh International translation and recitation audio, see the Qur’an studio in Lingua; to learn the Arabic itself, Bayan.
